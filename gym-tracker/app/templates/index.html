              Routine

              A routine is a timed sequence — work, rest, work — that the app counts you through. Add steps below, then press ▶ on the exercise to run it.

                Start from a template

                A ready-made challenge: the routines are built for you and any exercises they need are added to your library. Anything you already have is reused, never overwritten — and everything stays editable afterwards.

                New challenge

                A challenge is a set of items you tick off daily. Give it an end date to time-box it — a 30-day run finishes on its own and keeps its statistics. Leave the end date empty for an open-ended daily habit.

                On these days

                A day you finish earns its points; a due day you miss deducts the penalty once the day is over. Today is never counted against you while it is still open, and rest days are worth nothing either way.

                Challenge items

                Build this challenge from your exercises and supplements. Removing an item keeps your past streak intact.

                  Challenge history

                  Tap any dot to mark that day's item done or not. To backfill older records, set From back to when your data starts.

                  Reminders

                  Goal Tracker can nudge you through a Home Assistant notify service. Turn each reminder on and set its time on the add-on's Configuration tab; this screen shows their current status and lets you send a test.

                  Garmin Connect

                  Pull your sleep, stress, Body Battery and activities from Garmin. This is the one feature that talks to an external service; only a login token is stored locally — never your password.
